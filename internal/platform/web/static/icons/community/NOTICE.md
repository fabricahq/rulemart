# Community icons

Each icon here is a project's own logo, used where Devicon's version doesn't read at a group tile's size or Devicon
has none. Each entry says where the file came from and what Rulemart changed.

repository's MIT license. The project publishes no vector logo. Rulemart trimmed its transparent margin, centered the

## tanstack-query.svg and tanstack-router.svg

Each is TanStack's official mark, the rounded gradient square with the palm, from
[tanstack.com/favicon-light.svg](https://tanstack.com/favicon-light.svg) as of 2026-10-02, which TanStack offers on
its design system's [logos page](https://tanstack.com/ds/logos), used under that page's usage guidance: its
proportions and clear space are kept, and the mark reads on light and dark surfaces alike. Rulemart added a white circular badge at the
bottom right holding the glyph tanstack.com shows beside that library in its sidebar: a badge with a question mark
for TanStack Query, and a diamond with a turning arrow for TanStack Router. The glyphs are copied from tanstack.com's
sidebar SVGs; the badge's composition is Rulemart's.

## zustand.svg

The Zustand bear is Zustand's own logo ([pmndrs/zustand](https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand)). This one-color vector
of it was contributed by cicero-mello in
[pmndrs/zustand#1623](https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand/discussions/1623) and is used with the author's offer in that
thread. Rulemart drew its line art in `#5A4334`, rather than its `#AD9686`, over a body filled `#C9AE95`, so it
shows in color on the usual tile in both themes, as React's logo does. The body fill is a silhouette Rulemart traced
from the line art. Rulemart replaces it if the project publishes an official vector.
