## 🐛 Squashed Bugs

### The cart page works in production

v0.2.0's cart page didn't work in production. Behind CloudFront, the site refused the request that builds your checkout with a 403 error, so the cart page couldn't show your cart and checkout failed. CloudFront now accepts that request, so the cart page loads and checkout works. Deploying this release needs no infrastructure change, and it adds no migrations. (#46)
